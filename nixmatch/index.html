Privacy Policy — Nixmatch Gamebox

Effective date: October 13, 2026
Developer: Robert Cook / RAM Freight
Support / privacy contact: robert@ram-freight.com

Nixmatch Gamebox (“the App”), displayed as Nixmatch, is a collection of games for iPhone and iPad: Nixword, Chess, Checkers, and Nix Wits. This policy explains what data the App handles and how.

Summary

  • There is no Nixmatch account and no sign-up. The App has no Nixmatch server; we (the developer) do not receive your games, answers, or identity.
  • Online play uses Apple Game Center. Nearby play connects devices directly over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi.
  • The App shows ads from Google AdMob. Ads may use your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking in Apple’s prompt.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Ads (Google AdMob) and tracking

The App uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to show full-screen ads between matches and optional ads you choose to watch for coins. Ads never appear during a game. To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect device and usage information such as your IP address, device model, operating system, ad interactions, and, only if you tap “Allow” on Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt, your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA), which may be used to show more relevant ads across apps and websites.

If you tap “Ask App Not to Track”, the IDFA is not shared and you will still see ads, which may be less relevant. You can change your choice anytime in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, Google Privacy Policy, Google’s ad technology partners, and Google Ad Settings.

Buying Remove Ads turns off all ads between matches. Optional “+coins” ads stay available only if you choose to watch them.

In-app purchases (Apple StoreKit)

Coin packs and Remove Ads are sold through Apple’s App Store. Apple processes the payment under Apple’s Privacy Policy. We never receive your name, card, or billing details. The App receives only a signed record from Apple that a product was purchased, used on your device to add coins or turn off ads. Coins and the backgrounds they unlock are stored on your device.

Game Center

Online and friend games use Apple Game Center. Apple shares your Game Center display name and player ID with the App and with the players you play with, and Apple stores turn-based match data (moves, words, and Nix Wits answers for that match) on its servers to deliver turns. This is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy. You can play CPU games without signing in to Game Center.

Nearby play

Nearby games connect directly to devices close to you over Bluetooth and local Wi-Fi. Your device name or Game Center name and game moves are sent only to the players in that game.

Nix Wits user-written answers

  • Moderation: typed answers are filtered on your device before sending, blocking profanity, slurs, sexual content, links, emails, phone numbers and @handles. You must accept the Community Rules before playing.
  • Report & block: long-press any answer to report it; the answer is hidden for you immediately. You can block any player, and their answers are hidden from then on. You can also turn on “Hide typed answers” in Settings. To have a report reviewed by us, use the contact below; we act on reports within 24 hours and may remove offending players from future matches.
  • Retention: answers live only inside the match: on the players’ devices and, for online games, in Apple’s Game Center match data. Finished matches drop out of the App’s list after about 7 days. Your report and block lists are stored only on your device.
  • Deletion: deleting the App removes everything stored on your device (coins, settings, reports, blocks). Game Center match data is managed by Apple; you can quit or remove matches in Game Center. Email us for help with any request.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the App schedules “time almost up” reminders on your device. Game Center sends “your turn” alerts through Apple.

Backend

Nixmatch has no backend server of its own. Apart from Apple services (Game Center, App Store) and Google AdMob, the App makes no network requests.

Children’s privacy

The App is rated for ages 13 and up and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your choices

  • Choose “Ask App Not to Track” or change it later in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Buy Remove Ads to stop ads between matches.
  • Play CPU or nearby games without Game Center.
  • Turn off notifications, or hide typed Nix Wits answers, in Settings.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above will change when we do.
