Privacy Policy

Long-press the answer and tap Report answer; it’s hidden for you right away, and you can block the player. To have us review it, email robert@ram-freight.com with the answer text, the player’s Game Center name, and the time.

Common questions

  • Restore purchases: Settings › Shop & purchases › Restore Purchases.
  • Remove Ads: a one-time purchase that turns off all ads between matches.
  • What do coins buy? Background themes only. Coins never buy points or wins.
  • Can’t play friends? Sign in to Game Center in iPhone Settings › Game Center. CPU and nearby games work without it.
  • Tracking choice: iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Delete my data: delete the App to remove everything on your device, or email us for help.
