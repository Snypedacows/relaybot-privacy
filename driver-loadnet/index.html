Privacy Policy — TruckFlow IC: Budget Manager

Effective date: September 23, 2026
Developer: Robert Cook / RAM Freight
Support / privacy contact: driverloadnet@agentmail.to

TruckFlow IC: Budget Manager (“the App”), displayed as TruckFlow IC, is a trucking load profit and budget manager for independent contractors on iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what data the App handles and how.

Summary

  • The App is designed to work offline with data stored on your device.
  • We do not require an account.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not use advertising SDKs or cross-app tracking.

Data stored on your device

The App may store on-device: load calculations and history you save; truck profiles and fixed-cost schedules; calendar income, expenses, notes, and goals; preferences such as starting cash balance; and cached diesel price averages for fuel estimates. This information stays in the App’s on-device storage unless you delete the App or clear that data yourself.

Location

If you use Use current location for deadhead origin, the App requests location while using the App to reverse-geocode a city/state. Location is used only for that feature and is not uploaded to our servers by the App.

Photos / camera

If you take a photo with the camera or import load-board screenshots for on-device OCR, images are processed on your device to fill load fields. The App does not upload those photos to our servers.

Network requests

The App may fetch public diesel price averages from the U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA) over HTTPS to improve fuel estimates. That request does not include your loads, location, or identity.

Data sharing

We do not sell or share your load, calendar, or profile data with third parties for advertising.

Children’s privacy

The App is intended for professional trucking use and is not directed to children under 13.

Data retention & deletion

Data remains on your device until you delete entries in the App or delete the App. For privacy questions, email driverloadnet@agentmail.to.

Your choices

  • Deny location permission — type city/state instead.
  • Skip camera / screenshot import — enter fields manually.
  • Delete history, calendar entries, or truck profiles anytime in the App.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above will change when we do.
