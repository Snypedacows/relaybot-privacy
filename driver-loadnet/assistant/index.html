TruckFlow IC: use with an AI assistant

Developer: Robert Cook / RAM Freight
Support: driverloadnet@agentmail.to

An AI assistant can send a TruckFlow load card: a short block of text with the load details. The driver copies it and taps Paste Load from AI on the New Load screen (or screenshots it and picks the screenshot), and the load opens on the Confirm Load Scan screen for review. Nothing is saved until the driver saves it.

Instructions for your AI assistant

Copy the text below and give it to your AI assistant.

TruckFlow IC load card

Send the driver one load card per load, as plain text in a code block. The driver copies it and taps Paste Load from AI on the New Load screen (or screenshots it and picks the screenshot). TruckFlow opens it on the Confirm Load Scan screen for review and never saves it automatically. The driver checks every field and saves it himself.

Format (one field per line, exactly these labels, in this order):
TruckFlow Load
Pickup: City, ST
Delivery: City, ST
Pickup Date: MM/DD/YYYY HH:MM AM
Delivery Date: MM/DD/YYYY HH:MM PM
Loaded Miles: N
Deadhead: N
Deadhead From: City, ST
Linehaul: $0,000.00
FSC: $0,000.00
Weight: 00,000 lbs

Fields:
- Pickup / Delivery / Deadhead From: city, comma, 2-letter state, like Dallas, TX or St. Louis, MO. Deadhead From is where the empty miles start.
- Pickup Date / Delivery Date: MM/DD/YYYY with a 12-hour time and AM or PM, like 10/06/2026 08:00 AM.
- Loaded Miles / Deadhead: whole miles, numbers only.
- Linehaul: line rate without fuel surcharge, $ with cents, like $5,451.00.
- FSC: fuel surcharge, $ with cents, like $1,583.12.
- Weight: cargo weight in pounds, like 42,000 lbs.

Rules:
- Never put an all-in or total rate on the Linehaul line. If you only know the all-in rate, leave the Linehaul line out.
- Leave out any line you are not sure of. A missing value stays blank for the driver to fill in.
- Don't add other text inside the card.

Sample:
TruckFlow Load
Pickup: Dallas, TX
Delivery: St. Louis, MO
Pickup Date: 10/06/2026 08:00 AM
Delivery Date: 10/07/2026 02:00 PM
Loaded Miles: 630
Deadhead: 45
Deadhead From: Fort Worth, TX
Linehaul: $5,451.00
FSC: $1,583.12
Weight: 42,000 lbs
