Relaybot Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

What Relaybot is

Relaybot is an iPhone app that lets bots on your computer request actions on this device. You approve sensitive actions. Relaybot is built to keep decisions and logs on this iPhone unless you later connect a relay you control.

Data stored on this iPhone

  • Connection and Demo Mode session token (Keychain)
  • Whether you allowed bots, and which apps you granted
  • Job requests, approvals, denials, and the activity log
  • Ask Grok questions you choose to keep in Demo Mode
  • Device name you set for this iPhone

What we do not collect in this version

  • No analytics or advertising SDKs
  • No crash-reporting SDK
  • No sale of personal data
  • No microphone access for Ask Grok (keyboard dictation only)
  • No Accessibility / remote UI control of other apps
  • No silent sending of Messages or payments

Permissions

System permissions (for example Calendar write-only, or opening Maps / Shortcuts) are requested only when you approve an action that needs them. Purpose strings explain why. You can revoke access in iOS Settings.

Demo Mode

When Demo Mode is on, sign-in is local to this iPhone. Nothing is uploaded to a Relaybot server. A Demo Mode session is not Sign in with Apple and not a live third-party login.

Sharing

This build does not upload your activity log or grants. If you later connect a live relay, only the job results you approve will be sent to that relay under your account. We will not sell your data.

Your choices

  • Don’t Allow / Disconnect
  • Pause automation
  • Revoke per-app grants
  • Clear activity
  • Delete account & erase data on this iPhone (Settings) — removes Keychain session, grants, kept questions, and activity on device

Children

Relaybot is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Contact

Questions about privacy: relaybot@agentmail.to

Changes

When this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and in the in-app Privacy Policy screen.
