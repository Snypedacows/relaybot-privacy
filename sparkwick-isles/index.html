Privacy Policy — Sparkwick Isles: Math Quest

Effective date: November 14, 2026
Operator: Robert Cook
Support / privacy contact: sparkwickisles@agentmail.to
Support

Sparkwick Isles: Math Quest (“the App”) is a K–12 math game for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what data the App handles and how. “We,” “us,” and “our” mean Robert Cook, the operator of the App.

Summary

  • There is no account sign-up. We do not run a server for the App, and we do not receive your or your child’s information.
  • Progress, settings, and notes stay on the device and, if iCloud is on, sync only through the user’s own private iCloud.
  • The App has no ads, no analytics, no third-party software development kits (SDKs), and no tracking.
  • Kids cannot post, share, or send personal information to anyone. The App has no chat or social features, and no audio is recorded.
  • Apple (iCloud/CloudKit, StoreKit, ClassKit) is the only service that touches the data, and it protects the data with confidentiality and security protections equal to ours.
  • We do not sell personal information.

What the App stores

To save a student’s place in the game, the App stores the following on the device in Apple’s SwiftData:

  • Progress and mastery
  • Sparks, stickers, and streaks
  • Session notes, settings, and student-made saved notes (typed text and drawings)

Character voices are prerecorded audio files bundled in the App. The App does not record audio and does not use the microphone.

Where it is stored

  • On the device. The information above is saved inside the App on the device.
  • In your private iCloud (optional, automatic). When the device is signed in to iCloud, the same information syncs only through Apple CloudKit to the private iCloud database for that Apple Account, so it can appear on other devices using the same account. Only that account can read the private database; we cannot see it. If iCloud is not available, the information stays on the device only.

The App does not send this information to us or to any service other than Apple, as described below.

Apple services

Apple (iCloud/CloudKit, StoreKit, ClassKit) is the only service that touches the data, and it protects the data with confidentiality and security protections equal to ours. Those services are governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

  • iCloud / CloudKit. Used only for the private sync described above.
  • StoreKit. In-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your name, payment details, or Apple ID. The App receives only a record from Apple that a product was purchased. Purchases and outbound links sit behind a parent gate.
  • ClassKit / Schoolwork (schools only). If a school uses the App with Apple Schoolwork, optional teacher progress may be sent through Apple ClassKit to that school’s Schoolwork setup. That information goes to the school, not to us.

Mastery report

A grown-up can export a mastery report as a CSV file using the device’s share sheet. That export happens only when the grown-up asks for it, and it goes only where that person sends it. The App does not upload the report.

Children’s privacy (COPPA)

The App is made for children, including children under 13, and is intended to comply with the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

  • Kids cannot post, share, or send personal information to anyone. There are no chat or social features.
  • No audio is recorded, and the App does not use the microphone.
  • We do not collect personal information on our own servers. Information stays on the device and, if used, in the family’s or school’s Apple services described above.

Parents and guardians have the right to:

  • Review the information stored for their child, including in the App and by asking us at the contact below.
  • Delete that information, using the steps in “Deleting data” or by emailing us.
  • Refuse further collection of their child’s information, including by deleting the App, turning off iCloud for the App, or contacting us.

Deleting data

You can remove data in these ways:

  • On the device. Delete the App to remove the information stored on that device.
  • In iCloud. Open Settings, tap your name, then iCloud. Use Saved to iCloud or Manage Account Storage (the wording varies by iOS version), choose Sparkwick Isles, and delete the data. Deleting the App alone does not remove the iCloud copy.
  • By email. Email sparkwickisles@agentmail.to to request a review or deletion. We respond within 30 days. For a parent’s request about a child under 13, we act promptly.
  • School copies. For information a school has in Schoolwork, contact the school.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above will change when we do. We will not start collecting children’s personal information on our own servers without first updating this policy and meeting COPPA’s parental notice and consent rules.
