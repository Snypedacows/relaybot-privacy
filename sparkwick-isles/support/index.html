Privacy Policy

Sparkwick Isles: Math Quest is a K–12 math game for iPhone and iPad. If these answers don’t help, email us and include your device model and iOS version. Please don’t include a child’s real name or school details.

Common questions

  • Restore purchases: Purchases are sold through the App Store and sit behind a parent gate in the App. On a new device, sign in with the same Apple ID that made the purchase, then use Restore Purchases in the App.
  • Family Sharing: Purchases are made with your Apple ID. If your family uses Apple Family Sharing for App Store purchases, eligible purchases may be available to family members on their own devices. On a family member’s device, sign in with that person’s Apple ID and use Restore Purchases. If a purchase doesn’t appear, check that Family Sharing is on and that the purchase is set to be shared.
  • iCloud sync: Progress, mastery, Sparks, stickers, streaks, settings, and saved notes stay on the device. If the device is signed in to iCloud, that information syncs through your own private iCloud (CloudKit) to other devices using the same Apple Account. Without iCloud, it stays on that device only. We cannot see the private iCloud database. If sync is delayed, confirm both devices use the same Apple Account, that iCloud is on for the App, and that both are online.
  • School mode: If a school uses the App with Apple Schoolwork, teachers can receive optional progress through Apple ClassKit. That information goes to the school’s Schoolwork setup, not to us. Families using a regular App Store copy are not in a school ClassKit setup.
  • Reset or delete progress: Delete the App to remove data from that device. To also remove the iCloud copy, open Settings, tap your name, then iCloud. Use Saved to iCloud or Manage Account Storage (the wording varies by iOS version), choose Sparkwick Isles, and delete the data. You can also email sparkwickisles@agentmail.to for help. We respond within 30 days, and we act promptly on a parent’s request about a child under 13.
